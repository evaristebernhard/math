\section{Persistence, stability, and critical residence}\label{sec:consequences}
Write $\theta=(A,B,\mu)$ and $\theta_0=(9,3/2,181363/100000)$.
Use subscripts $\theta$ for the flow $x_\theta(t;y)$ and the
return displacement $D_\theta(y)=x_\theta(2\pi;y)-y$.

\begin{proposition}[Explicit parameter cube preserving six signs]
\label{prop:quantitative}
For every triple satisfying
\begin{equation}\label{eq:param-cube}
 |A-9|<10^{-12},\quad |B-3/2|<10^{-12},\quad
 |\mu-181363/100000|<10^{-12},
\end{equation}
all six displacement signs of Table~\ref{tab:signs} remain strict,
and there are at least seven distinct $2\pi$-periodic trajectories.
This statement alone does not certify isolation throughout the
whole explicit cube.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Put $f_\theta(x,t)=-Ax+(A+B)\sat(x)+\mu\sin t$.
The reference field $f_{\theta_0}$ points inward at $x=\pm2$,
as $15/2-\mu_0>0$. Therefore $|x_{\theta_0}(t;y)|\le2$
for $|y|\le2$ and $t\ge0$. Each secant slope of
$f_\theta(\cdot,t)$ is at most $B<2$; its affine slopes
are $-A$ and $B$. Along $x_0=x_{\theta_0}(t;y)$,
\[
 |f_\theta(x_0,t)-f_{\theta_0}(x_0,t)|
 \le |\Delta A|(|x_0|+|\sat(x_0)|)
 +|\Delta B|\,|\sat(x_0)|+|\Delta\mu|
 <5\cdot10^{-12}.
\]
The one-sided Gronwall inequality between the two trajectories,
started at the same $y$, gives
\begin{equation}\label{eq:uniform-parameter}
 |x_\theta(t;y)-x_{\theta_0}(t;y)|
 \le5\cdot10^{-12}\frac{e^{2t}-1}{2}
 <1.5\cdot10^{-6},\quad 0\le t\le2\pi,\quad |y|\le2.
\end{equation}
Here $\pi<13/4$ and $e<11/4$ imply
$e^{4\pi}<e^{13}<(11/4)^{13}<600000$.
The smallest absolute displacement margin in
Table~\ref{tab:signs} exceeds $4.96788\cdot10^{-6}$,
so all six signs survive. By continuity the three intervals
\eqref{eq:intervals} retain fixed points.

Half-period reflection still gives a unique central solution.
Since $Q_{\theta_0}(-0.571)>0.01911577$ by
\eqref{eq:Qbound}, the bound at $t=\pi$ implies
$Q_\theta(-0.571)>0$, placing the central initial value
below $I_1$. Reflection supplies three distinct companions
below it, for a total of at least seven periodic trajectories.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Robust isolation and alternating stability]
\label{thm:robust}
There is $\varepsilon_*\in(0,10^{-12}]$ such that
\[
 |A-9|,\quad |B-3/2|,\quad|\mu-\mu_0|<\varepsilon_*,
 \qquad \mu_0=181363/100000,
\]
implies at least seven isolated periodic orbits, of which
at least four are locally attracting and at least three
are locally repelling. This does not assert hyperbolicity
of the selected noncentral orbits.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
At $\theta_0$ the strict grazing barriers in
Section~\ref{sec:proof} exclude tangencies for all
$y\in I_1\cup I_2\cup I_3$; the crossings are transverse,
and no endpoint lies on a switching level.
By compactness and continuous dependence, these uniform
strict conditions persist for sufficiently small perturbations
of $\theta$. Hence $D_\theta$ is real analytic in $y$
on neighborhoods of each closed interval $I_j$.
Strict opposite endpoint signs prevent it from being
identically zero. Its zeros in each $I_j$ are finite
and isolated.

An analytic displacement starting positive and ending
negative has at least one zero changing sign from
positive to negative. The scalar return map is strictly
increasing, so iterates of nearby states converge
monotonically to this fixed point: it is attracting.
Likewise a negative-to-positive change yields a
repelling fixed point. The patterns in
Table~\ref{tab:signs} are $(+,-)$, $(-,+)$, $(+,-)$,
giving two attracting and one repelling cycles above
the central solution.

The central solution is hyperbolic repelling at
$\theta_0$ by \eqref{eq:centerhyper}. Its initial value
is the unique zero of the strictly increasing
$Q_\theta(y)=x_\theta(\pi;y)+y$ and depends continuously
on the parameters. The central transverse itinerary
and its multiplier persist on a smaller neighborhood,
so it remains repelling and isolated.
The half-period symmetry $x(t)\mapsto-x(t+\pi)$ preserves
local stability and yields distinct reflected orbits
below the central solution. Altogether this selects
four attracting and three repelling isolated orbits.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Three critical residence-time profiles]
\label{cor:critical}
At the witness parameters the biased mean response
has at least three distinct positive-mean stationary
profiles satisfying
\[
 \Lambda_\xi(\xi,\mu_0)=0,\qquad T(\xi,\mu_0)=\rho.
\]
The conclusion persists in a sufficiently small
parameter neighborhood, with the quantities evaluated
at the perturbed parameters.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
Theorem~\ref{thm:seven} supplies ordered solutions above
the antisymmetric central solution, with means
$0<\xi_1<\xi_2<\xi_3$. The central mean is zero,
and $\Lambda(0,\mu_0)=\Lambda(\xi_i,\mu_0)=0$.
Rolle's theorem on $(0,\xi_1)$, $(\xi_1,\xi_2)$
and $(\xi_2,\xi_3)$ gives three distinct stationary
responses. Equation~\eqref{eq:key}, including its
continuous extension at isolated grazings, gives
$T=\rho$ for each. The same argument applies to
Theorem~\ref{thm:robust} at nearby parameters.
No nondegeneracy or exact global fold count follows.
\end{proof}

Proposition~\ref{prop:quantitative} certifies an explicit
cube for distinct periodic trajectories. The neighborhood
of isolated cycles in Theorem~\ref{thm:robust} is existential:
an explicit uniform box for the grazing barriers and central
multiplier remains to be computed.
The script \texttt{proof/certify\_parameter\_persistence.py}
checks the elementary parameter estimate against the
original interval endpoint certificate; it is not
an independent second interval-arithmetic backend.
