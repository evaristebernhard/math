# Cover letter draft — International Journal of Foundations of Computer Science

Dear Editors of the *International Journal of Foundations of Computer Science*,

We would like to submit the manuscript

**“Binary Words with Few Abelian Squares: Four and Five Occurrences of a Letter and Run-Length Structure”**

for consideration as a research article in the *International Journal of Foundations of Computer Science*.

The paper studies the minimum number of distinct Abelian-square factors in binary words with a prescribed Parikh vector. Building on the fixed-Parikh formulation of Fazekas, Mammoliti, Mercaş, and Simpson, we determine the next two fixed-minority cases after those treated in their work. For binary words of length \(n\), we determine \(M(4,n)\) and \(M(5,n)\) exactly and classify all minimising words. In the five-occurrence case we also prove a run-rigidity result showing that a minimiser has at most two runs of the minority letter.

The manuscript also gives several results for positive-gap run-length encodings: an exact rank-window criterion for Abelian-square factors, a lower bound for weakly monotone gap sequences, and the conjectured fixed-Parikh lower bound for positive-gap words having no Abelian-square factor containing four occurrences of the minority letter. The paper does not claim the general fixed-Parikh conjecture.

All proofs are contained in the manuscript. A supplementary verifier is included only as an independent finite check of the exact formulae, classifications, and auxiliary lemmas; the proofs do not depend on computation.

The subject lies within formal-language and combinatorial aspects of theoretical computer science, in particular combinatorics on finite words.
