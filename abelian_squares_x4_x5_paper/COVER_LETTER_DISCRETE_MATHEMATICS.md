# Cover letter draft — Discrete Mathematics

Dear Editors of *Discrete Mathematics*,

We would like to submit the manuscript

**“Binary Words with Few Abelian Squares: Four and Five Occurrences of a Letter and Run-Length Structure”**

for consideration as a research article in *Discrete Mathematics*.

The paper studies the minimum number of distinct Abelian-square factors in binary words with a prescribed Parikh vector. Building on the recent fixed-Parikh formulation of Fazekas, Mammoliti, Mercaş, and Simpson, we determine the next two fixed-minority cases after those treated in their work. For binary words of length \(n\), we determine \(M(4,n)\) and \(M(5,n)\) exactly and classify all minimising words. In the five-occurrence case we also prove a run-rigidity result showing that a minimiser has at most two runs of the minority letter.

The paper also records several consequences for positive-gap run-length encodings. These include an exact rank-window criterion for Abelian-square factors, a lower bound for weakly monotone gap sequences, and the conjectured fixed-Parikh lower bound for positive-gap words having no Abelian-square factor containing four occurrences of the minority letter.

All proofs are given in the manuscript. A supplementary verifier is included only for independent finite checks of the exact formulae, classifications, and auxiliary lemmas; the proofs do not depend on computation.

We submit the paper to *Discrete Mathematics* because its subject is combinatorics on finite words and an extremal counting problem for discrete structures.
